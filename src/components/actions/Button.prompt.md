Primary action trigger; use a specific verb label ("Agendar diagnóstico", not "Enviar").

```jsx
<Button iconRight="arrow-right">Agendar diagnóstico</Button>
<Button variant="secondary">Ver casos</Button>
<Button variant="tertiary" iconLeft="rotate-ccw">Reintentar</Button>
<Button loading>Guardando</Button>
```

- `variant`: primary (lime, max one per view) · secondary (outline) · tertiary (text) · inverse · danger.
- `size`: sm 32 · md 40 · lg 48. Radius 6px.
- `href` renders an anchor.
